#### **Citation Networks & Authorship Networks** 

Task 4 — Map the knowledge graph within and across your assigned institutions 

###### **<mark>Citation Network</mark>** 

How knowledge flows between papers across institutions 

###### **<mark>Authorship Network</mark>** 

Who collaborates with whom across institutions 

- → Extract paper-cites-paper pairs from OpenAlex / Scopus 

- → Build a directed graph: nodes = papers, edges = citations 

- → Compute in-degree (cited-by count) per paper 

- → Compute PageRank to find influential papers 

- → Map citation flow into and out of your institutions 

- → Identify papers with cross-institute citations 

- → Export as edge list: source_doi, target_doi, year 

- → Visualise as directed network (Gephi / NetworkX / D3) 

   - → Extract co-author pairs from every paper in your dataset 

   - → Build an undirected graph: nodes = authors, edges = co-authorship 

   - → Compute degree centrality (most collaborative faculty) 

   - → Compute betweenness centrality (brokers between clusters) 

   - → Apply community detection (Louvain / Leiden algorithm) 

   - → Flag cross-institution edges — these are Indian connections 

   - → Export as edge list: author_a, author_b, paper_doi, year 

   - → Visualise collaboration clusters in your institution(s) 

- ⚠ Both networks must be submitted as edge-list CSV files alongside your main dataset. See slide 12 for required columns.
