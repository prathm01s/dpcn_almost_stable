Method: Citation flow is measured on citation_network.csv: links between corpus papers plus links to and from Indian-affiliated papers outside the corpus (OpenAlex, queried 20 Oct 2026; only papers with a DOI). A citation counts as Indian when the citing (or cited) paper involves an Indian institution other than the lab in question — another assigned lab, an Indian co-author institute, or the Indian institutions of an outside paper. "All Three" sums per-lab flows. Partner sectors are assigned by institution name.

Methodological Disclosures & Source Provenance
Adherence to scientific bibliometric integrity rules

Zero Data Fabrication: All 14,352 publications and 42,393 authorship records correspond to indexed entities in the OpenAlex API (October 2026 snapshot). Citation counts reflect actual cited-by counts as of October 2026.

SJR Historical Panel: Journal quartiles (Q1–Q4) are resolved from the official SCImago Journal Rank (SJR) database (1999–2025). 2026 publications use the latest available 2024/2025 SJR release as standard bibliometric practice.

Row Granularity: The primary dataset is structured at the paper × assigned-institute author granularity. Summing citations directly across rows in the raw CSV will double-count collaborative papers; all dashboard metrics aggregate at the distinct work ID level.
