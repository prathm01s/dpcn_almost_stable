Data, Downloads & Methods

The submitted files, the report, and the rules behind every number on this page.

Data checks: author names and affiliations
What was checked, how many cases, and how each is handled.
Data sources, rankings and citation database
Where every number comes from.
  • Papers, authors, affiliations, citations, references: OpenAlex (openalex.org), collected 13 Oct 2026; Indian citing and cited papers queried 20 Oct 2026. Citation counts are OpenAlex cited-by counts.
  • Journal quartiles (Q1–Q4): SCImago Journal Rank (SJR, Scopus data), best quartile of the paper's year; 2026 papers use 2025.
  • Journal impact factor (IF): SCImago "Cites / Doc. (2 years)" of the journal in the paper's year — a Scopus-based analogue; Clarivate JCR impact factors are paywalled and not used.
  • Conference ranks: CORE portal (computer-science venues; chemistry conferences are mostly unranked).
  • Faculty rosters: official CSIR-NCL, CSIR-IICT and CSIR-CECRI websites; matched to OpenAlex authors; technical staff removed.
  • Institution identities and sectors: ROR and OpenAlex institution records; sectors by name rules, corrected in config/overrides/sector_overrides.csv.

Methodological Disclosures & Source Provenance
Adherence to scientific bibliometric integrity rules

No invented data: all – papers and – paper × lab-author rows are OpenAlex records (collected 13 Oct 2026). Citation counts are OpenAlex cited-by counts on that date. Duplicate records of the same paper (e.g. the German edition of Angewandte Chemie, repository copies, version DOIs) and non-research items (retraction notices, cover pictures, prefaces) were removed; the list is in data/review/duplicate_papers_removed.csv.

Networks: the faculty networks have one node per roster scientist (– with ≥1 paper), keyed by an internal ID so that a person with several OpenAlex profiles or name spellings is one node, and two people with the same name stay two nodes. The paper citation network has one node per paper; the all-author co-authorship network includes PhD students, postdocs and project staff.

SJR Historical Panel: Journal quartiles (Q1–Q4) are resolved from the official SCImago Journal Rank (SJR) database (1999–2025). 2026 publications use the latest available 2024/2025 SJR release as standard bibliometric practice.

Row Granularity: The primary dataset is structured at the paper × assigned-institute author granularity. Summing citations directly across rows in the raw CSV will double-count collaborative papers; all dashboard metrics aggregate at the distinct work ID level.
