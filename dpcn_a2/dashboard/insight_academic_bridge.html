The Academic Bridge: Do the CSIR Labs Export or Import Knowledge?

The directional Knowledge Exchange Ratio ($K_{ER}$) compares, for each of 15 Indian institutions (IITs, IISc and state universities), how often its papers cite the three CSIR labs with how often the labs' papers cite it. Counts come from data/final/citation_network.csv: links between the labs' papers and DOI-bearing papers of other Indian institutions (OpenAlex).

Primary Data Sources

  • Citation Network: data/final/citation_network.csv: directed paper-to-paper links with source DOI, target DOI, source institute(s), target institute(s) and year; only links where one side is a paper of the three labs and the other names one of the 15 institutions are counted.
  • Indian Institutional Connections: data/final/indian_connections.csv recording joint papers and shared citations across 150+ Indian universities.
  • Institutional Categorization: Grouped into Tier-1 (Legacy IITs + IISc) and Tier-2 (Regional State/Central Universities).

Mathematical Formulations

$$K_{ER}(i, j) = \frac{C_{j \to i}}{C_{i \to j}} = \frac{\text{Citations from HEI } j \text{ to CSIR } i}{\text{Citations from CSIR } i \text{ to HEI } j}$$

Knowledge Exchange Ratio ($K_{ER}$): Values $> 1.0$ indicate Net Knowledge Exporters (CSIR feeds academic research). Values $< 1.0$ indicate Net Knowledge Importers.

$$\Delta C(i, j) = C_{j \to i} - C_{i \to j}$$

Preprocessing & Exclusion Rules

  • Self-Citations Excluded: Internal cross-citations within the same lab are isolated to evaluate genuine inter-institutional diffusion.
  • Affiliation Disambiguation: Standardized OpenAlex institution strings for IITs, IISc, and state universities to prevent split-campus false negatives.
